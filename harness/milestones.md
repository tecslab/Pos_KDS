# Development Milestones

Milestones guide task selection; task dependencies remain authoritative.

## M1 — Foundation and Domain Infrastructure

T-001 through T-021.

Outcome: application skeleton, quality tooling, configuration, persistence models, invariants, shared domain services, realtime boundary, and printing boundary.

## M2 — Identity and Administration

T-022 through T-034.

Outcome: authentication, authorization enforcement, application shell, and configuration/catalog administration.

## M3 — Point of Sale and Order Management

T-035 through T-046 plus T-079.

Outcome: draft composition, confirmation, inventory-aware editing, active-order queries, and cancellation.

## M4 — Kitchen, Delivery, and Payments

T-047 through T-057.

Outcome: live kitchen and delivery workflows, valid state transitions, split/partial settlement, and receipts.

## M5 — Inventory and Production

T-058 through T-065.

Outcome: purchases, immutable adjustments/waste, alerts, production, and operational history.

## M6 — Reporting and Release Quality

T-066 through T-078.

Outcome: operational reports, exports, workflow coverage, security regression testing, observability, performance, accessibility, deployment, backup, and printer readiness.

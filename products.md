## Notes From Code

- `alias` is used by the thermal printer when present; otherwise the product `name` is printed.
- `variedad` is used by the PoS menu for category-specific product labels. Especiales and Bebidas do not define `variedad`.
- `ingredientes.eliminable: true` means the ingredient can be removed by the user and shown/printed as a product detail.
- `ingredientes.eliminable: false` means the ingredient is fixed in the product definition.
- Only `Taco Pollo Asado` specifies an ingredient quantity: Aguacate, `25 gramos`.
- Burritos define the `Dividido` option with default `false`.
- Products without an `ingredientes` array have no ingredient composition specified in the catalog constants.

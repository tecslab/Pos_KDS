# 5. Functional Requirements

This section describes the core functional capabilities of the Restaurant Management System. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, every module must operate in real time and synchronize changes across all connected clients.

---

# 5.1 Point of Sale (PoS)

## Purpose

The Point of Sale (PoS) is the primary interface used by waiters to register customer orders, modify active orders, manage split accounts, and process payments.

The PoS is the entry point of the restaurant workflow. Every confirmed order originates from this module.

The interface must be optimized for tablet usage and minimize the number of interactions required to complete an order.

---

## Primary Users

* Waiters
* Administrators (optional)

---

## Functional Requirements

### FR-POS-001 — Create Order

The system shall allow a waiter to create a new order by selecting:

* Service location (table or dispatch window)
* Customer basket (General or specific customer)
* Menu items
* Quantity
* Product modifications
* Optional observations

The system shall display the current order summary while it is being built.

---

### FR-POS-002 — Table Configuration

The system shall support configurable service locations.

Each location shall define:

* Name
* Type (Table, Window, Counter, etc.)
* Whether multiple simultaneous active orders are allowed

Example:

| Location        | Multiple Active Orders |
| --------------- | ---------------------- |
| Table 5         | No                     |
| Dispatch Window | Yes                    |

This behavior shall be configurable without modifying application code.

---

### FR-POS-003 — Product Selection

Products shall be organized by category.

Each product shall include:

* Name
* Price
* Printer alias
* Available ingredient removals
* Available options
* Recipe reference
* Tax configuration

The menu shall be managed from the administration module rather than being hardcoded.

---

### FR-POS-004 — Product Modifications

Users shall be able to customize products according to their predefined configuration.

Supported modifications include:

* Removing ingredients
* Selecting product options
* Entering free-text observations (optional)

Only modifications allowed by the product definition shall be available.

Product modifications shall not change the product price unless explicitly configured.

---

### FR-POS-005 — Split Accounts

The system shall allow products to be assigned to individual customer baskets.

An order may contain:

* One shared basket
* Multiple individual customer baskets
* Both simultaneously

This allows customers to:

* Share common items
* Pay individually for personal items

The system shall calculate subtotals for each basket independently.

---

### FR-POS-006 — Edit Active Orders

Pending orders may be modified after confirmation.

Supported modifications include:

* Add products
* Remove products
* Modify quantities
* Change observations
* Change product modifications

Every modification shall generate an audit record.

Kitchen displays shall automatically receive the updated information.

---

### FR-POS-007 — Order Confirmation

When an order is confirmed, the system shall:

* Persist the order immediately.
* Generate a unique order number.
* Send the order to the Kitchen Display System.
* Register the initial order status as **Pending**.
* Print the kitchen ticket (if printing is enabled).
* Update dashboards in real time.

Orders shall never exist exclusively in memory.

---

### FR-POS-008 — Order Persistence

Orders shall be stored immediately after confirmation.

The system shall never wait until payment or completion before persisting order information.

This guarantees:

* Crash recovery
* Reporting accuracy
* Auditability
* Reliable synchronization

---

### FR-POS-009 — Order Cancellation

Only users with the appropriate permission may cancel an order.

Cancellation shall:

* Require confirmation.
* Record the user performing the action.
* Record the cancellation reason.
* Roll back inventory movements generated by the order.
* Generate an audit event.

Canceled orders shall remain visible in historical reports.

---

### FR-POS-010 — Real-Time Synchronization

Every confirmed or modified order shall immediately synchronize with:

* Kitchen Display System
* Waiter Delivery Panel
* Payment module
* Reporting dashboards

No manual refresh shall be required.

---

## Business Rules

* Orders are always associated with exactly one service location.
* Products with identical configurations shall be grouped into a single order line.
* Product modifications affect preparation instructions but do not alter prices unless configured.
* Orders may be edited until canceled or fully paid.
* All order changes must be auditable.

---

# 5.2 Kitchen Display System (KDS)

## Purpose

The Kitchen Display System (KDS) provides kitchen personnel with a real-time queue of pending orders.

The interface is optimized for fast visual recognition and minimal interaction.

---

## Primary Users

* Kitchen Personnel

---

## Functional Requirements

### FR-KDS-001 — Receive Orders

Confirmed orders shall automatically appear in the kitchen queue.

No manual refresh shall be required.

---

### FR-KDS-002 — Kitchen Card

Each kitchen card shall display:

* Order number
* Service location
* Order creation time
* Elapsed preparation time
* Products
* Quantities
* Product modifications
* Observations

---

### FR-KDS-003 — Preparation Timer

Each order shall display an elapsed preparation timer.

The timer shall continue until the order is marked as **Ready**.

---

### FR-KDS-004 — Visual Priority

Orders waiting longer than configurable thresholds shall be visually highlighted.

Default thresholds:

* Green: Normal
* Yellow: Warning
* Red: Critical

Threshold values shall be configurable.

---

### FR-KDS-005 — Ready Status

Kitchen personnel shall mark an order as **Ready** once preparation has finished.

Changing the status shall:

* Remove the order from the active preparation queue.
* Notify all waiter devices.
* Update dashboards.
* Record preparation completion time.

---

### FR-KDS-006 — Live Synchronization

Every kitchen screen shall remain synchronized with all others.

Status changes shall be immediately reflected on every connected device.

---

## Business Rules

* Orders are processed in confirmation order unless manually prioritized.
* Kitchen personnel cannot modify products or prices.
* Kitchen personnel cannot process payments.
* Kitchen personnel cannot cancel orders.

---

# 5.3 Waiter Delivery Panel

## Purpose

The Waiter Delivery Panel helps waiters manage prepared orders awaiting delivery.

This module reduces communication delays between the kitchen and waiters while providing visibility into delivery performance.

This functionality is considered a key differentiator of the application.

---

## Primary Users

* Waiters

---

## Functional Requirements

### FR-WDP-001 — Ready Queue

The panel shall display every order currently in the **Ready** state.

Each card shall include:

* Order number
* Table
* Waiting time
* Number of products
* Special observations

---

### FR-WDP-002 — Mark "On the Way"

When a waiter picks up an order from the kitchen, the order shall transition to:

**On the Way**

The system shall record:

* User
* Timestamp

---

### FR-WDP-003 — Mark "Delivered"

After all products belonging to an order have reached the customer, the waiter shall mark the order as:

**Delivered**

The system shall record:

* Delivery time
* Delivering waiter

---

### FR-WDP-004 — Waiting Indicators

Orders waiting for delivery longer than configurable thresholds shall be visually highlighted.

---

### FR-WDP-005 — Search and Filtering

Waiters shall be able to filter the list by:

* Table
* Order number
* Waiting time

---

### FR-WDP-006 — Performance Metrics

The system shall collect delivery metrics including:

* Time between Ready and On the Way
* Time between On the Way and Delivered
* Total delivery duration

These metrics shall be available for reporting.

---

## Business Rules

* Only waiters may change an order to **On the Way** or **Delivered**.
* An order cannot be marked as Delivered before reaching the On the Way state.
* Delivered indicates that every product belonging to the order has reached the customer.
* The On the Way and Delivered states are operational workflow states intended to improve restaurant productivity and measure service efficiency.

---

# 5.4 Payment System

## Purpose

The Payment System manages the financial settlement of customer orders while supporting split accounts and partial payments.

Order fulfillment and payment are treated as independent business processes.

---

## Primary Users

* Waiters
* Administrators

---

## Functional Requirements

### FR-PAY-001 — View Pending Payments

The system shall display every unpaid order.

Each order shall include:

* Customer baskets
* Outstanding balance
* Payment history

---

### FR-PAY-002 — Split Payments

Each customer basket may be paid independently.

A payment shall affect only the selected basket.

The order shall remain unpaid until every basket has been fully settled.

---

### FR-PAY-003 — Partial Payments

Customer baskets shall support partial payments.

The remaining balance shall be updated after every payment.

---

### FR-PAY-004 — Payment Methods

The initial release shall support:

* Cash
* Bank Transfer (Bank A)
* Bank Transfer (Bank B)

Additional payment methods shall be configurable in future releases.

---

### FR-PAY-005 — Payment Validation

The system shall prevent payments that exceed the outstanding balance unless explicitly authorized by a privileged user.

---

### FR-PAY-006 — Payment History

Every payment shall record:

* Amount
* Payment method
* User
* Timestamp
* Optional reference number
* Optional comments

Payment records shall never be deleted.

---

### FR-PAY-007 — Order Completion

An order shall automatically transition to **Paid** only after every customer basket has been fully paid.

---

### FR-PAY-008 — Receipts

The system shall support printing payment receipts using the configured thermal printer.

Receipt formatting shall be configurable.

---

## Business Rules

* Orders may remain in the Delivered state while awaiting payment.
* Payments do not modify order contents.
* Payments are irreversible unless refunded through an authorized administrative operation.
* Every payment shall be fully auditable.
* The system shall calculate taxes as part of the product price. The initial deployment assumes all menu prices include a 15% tax.
* Payment information shall be permanently linked to its corresponding order for reporting and auditing purposes.


# 5.5 Inventory Management

## Purpose

The Inventory Management module maintains accurate stock levels for every inventory item while providing complete traceability of all inventory movements.

Rather than directly modifying stock quantities, the system records inventory events that collectively determine the current stock level. This approach provides a complete audit trail and allows inventory discrepancies to be investigated.

ax management is designed to support both ingredients consumed during production and products sold directly without prior production.

---

## Primary Users

* Administrator
* Manager (future role)

---

## Inventory Model

The system shall support three inventory item types:

| Type           | Description                                                                  |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Raw Ingredient | Consumed during production (e.g., meat, tortillas, vegetables).              |
| Produced Item  | Created through a production process and later sold (e.g., prepared sauces). |
| Resale Item    | Purchased and sold directly without production (e.g., bottled drinks).       |

Each inventory item shall define:

* Name
* Unit of measurement
* Inventory type
* Minimum stock level
* Current stock
* Active status

---

## Functional Requirements

### FR-INV-001 — Inventory Items

The system shall allow administrators to create, edit, activate and deactivate inventory items.

Inventory items may be referenced by:

* Product recipes
* Purchases
* Production records
* Sales
* Adjustments
* Waste records

Inventory items shall not be permanently deleted once referenced by operational data.

---

### FR-INV-002 — Inventory Movements

Every inventory change shall generate an immutable inventory movement.

Supported movement types include:

* Purchase
* Production Consumption
* Production Output
* Sale
* Adjustment
* Waste
* Rollback

Each movement shall record:

* Item
* Quantity
* Unit
* Movement type
* User
* Timestamp
* Business reference
* Optional comments

Inventory balances shall be calculated from these movements.

---

### FR-INV-003 — Purchases

Administrators shall be able to register purchases of inventory items.

Purchases shall:

* Increase inventory.
* Record supplier information (optional).
* Record purchase price.
* Record quantity.
* Generate an inventory movement.
* Generate the corresponding operating expense.

---

### FR-INV-004 — Inventory Adjustments

Authorized users shall be able to adjust inventory after physical counts.

Adjustment records shall require:

* Quantity
* Reason
* User
* Timestamp

Historical adjustments shall never be modified or deleted.

---

### FR-INV-005 — Waste Registration

The system shall support recording inventory losses caused by:

* Expired products
* Damaged products
* Kitchen waste
* Operational incidents

Waste records shall decrease inventory and remain permanently auditable.

---

### FR-INV-006 — Automatic Sales Consumption

Products classified as **Resale Items** shall automatically decrease inventory when sold.

No production process shall be required for these products.

Example:

* Soft drinks
* Bottled water
* Beer

---

### FR-INV-007 — Inventory Availability

The system shall prevent inventory from becoming negative unless explicitly authorized through a configurable administrative setting.

---

### FR-INV-008 — Inventory Alerts

The system shall notify administrators when inventory falls below configured minimum levels.

Alerts shall be displayed on dashboards and administration screens.

---

## Business Rules

* Inventory shall never be modified directly without generating an inventory movement.
* Every movement shall be permanently auditable.
* Inventory reservations are not supported.
* Inventory shall be updated only after the business event that consumes or generates stock occurs.
* Historical inventory movements shall never be edited.

---

# 5.6 Production Management

## Purpose

The Production module records the transformation of raw ingredients into sellable products.

Production is independent of customer orders and reflects the restaurant's preparation process.

---

## Primary Users

* Administrator
* Manager (future role)

---

## Functional Requirements

### FR-PROD-001 — Recipes

The system shall allow products to define one or more recipes.

Each recipe shall contain:

* Ingredients
* Quantities
* Units
* Produced quantity

Recipes shall be versioned to preserve historical accuracy.

---

### FR-PROD-002 — Register Production

Administrators shall register production batches.

The system shall:

* Consume recipe ingredients.
* Increase stock of the produced item.
* Generate inventory movements.
* Record production history.

---

### FR-PROD-003 — Production Validation

Production shall only be allowed when sufficient ingredients are available.

If insufficient stock exists, the operation shall be rejected.

---

### FR-PROD-004 — Production History

Each production batch shall record:

* Produced product
* Quantity
* Recipe version
* User
* Timestamp
* Optional notes

---

### FR-PROD-005 — Recipe Management

Recipes may be edited over time.

Historical production records shall remain linked to the recipe version used at production time.

---

## Business Rules

* Production consumes raw ingredients.
* Production creates produced items.
* Sales never consume raw ingredients directly unless the product is classified as a resale item.
* Inventory consumption occurs during production, not during customer sales.
* Every production operation shall generate auditable inventory movements.

---

# 5.7 Reports & Analytics

## Purpose

The Reporting module provides operational, financial and inventory insights that assist restaurant management in monitoring daily performance and making business decisions.

Reports are intended for operational analysis rather than accounting compliance.

---

## Primary Users

* Administrator
* Manager (future role)

---

## Functional Requirements

### FR-REP-001 — Sales Dashboard

The system shall provide a daily sales dashboard displaying:

* Total revenue
* Orders created
* Orders completed
* Average ticket
* Revenue by hour

---

### FR-REP-002 — Product Performance

The system shall report:

* Best-selling products
* Sales by category
* Product quantities sold
* Revenue by product

---

### FR-REP-003 — Kitchen Performance

The system shall calculate:

* Average preparation time
* Longest preparation time
* Orders currently in preparation
* Peak preparation periods

---

### FR-REP-004 — Waiter Performance

The system shall provide delivery metrics including:

* Average delivery time
* Orders delivered
* Orders waiting for delivery
* Delivery bottlenecks

These reports are intended to evaluate operational efficiency rather than employee performance.

---

### FR-REP-005 — Payment Reports

Reports shall include:

* Revenue by payment method
* Outstanding balances
* Partial payments
* Payment history

---

### FR-REP-006 — Inventory Reports

The system shall report:

* Current inventory
* Inventory movements
* Low-stock items
* Purchases
* Production
* Waste
* Inventory adjustments

---

### FR-REP-007 — Expense Reports

The system shall provide visibility into:

* Operating expenses
* Purchases
* Expense categories
* Daily and monthly expense summaries

---

### FR-REP-008 — Exporting

Reports shall support export to common business formats such as PDF and spreadsheet files.

---

## Business Rules

* Reports shall be generated from persisted data only.
* Historical information shall remain available after orders are completed or canceled.
* Reports shall respect user permissions.
* Report calculations shall remain reproducible from historical data.

---

# 5.8 Administration

## Purpose

The Administration module centralizes system configuration and operational management.

It provides authorized users with tools to configure the restaurant without modifying application code.

---

## Primary Users

* Administrator

---

## Functional Requirements

### FR-ADM-001 — User Management

The system shall integrate with Supabase Authentication.

Administrators shall be able to:

* Invite users.
* Activate or deactivate users.
* Assign roles.
* Reset user access.

Authentication shall be delegated to Supabase.

Authorization shall be managed by the application.

---

### FR-ADM-002 — Role Management

The system shall implement Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).

Permissions shall be assigned to roles instead of individual users.

The permission model shall support future role expansion.

---

### FR-ADM-003 — Product Catalog

Administrators shall manage:

* Categories
* Products
* Prices
* Printer aliases
* Available modifications
* Tax configuration
* Active status

The menu shall be configurable without software updates.

---

### FR-ADM-004 — Location Management

Administrators shall configure restaurant service locations.

Each location shall define:

* Name
* Type
* Display order
* Whether multiple active orders are allowed

This configuration shall support future expansion without application changes.

---

### FR-ADM-005 — Payment Configuration

Administrators shall configure:

* Available payment methods
* Payment method activation
* Bank account information
* Receipt configuration

---

### FR-ADM-006 — System Configuration

Administrators shall configure operational parameters including:

* Preparation warning thresholds
* Delivery warning thresholds
* Tax rates
* Inventory policies
* Printing behavior
* Business hours

---

### FR-ADM-007 — Audit Log

The system shall maintain a centralized audit log.

Auditable events include, but are not limited to:

* User authentication
* Role changes
* Order modifications
* Order cancellations
* Inventory movements
* Production
* Payments
* Administrative configuration changes

Audit records shall include:

* User
* Timestamp
* Entity
* Action
* Previous values (when applicable)
* New values (when applicable)

Audit information shall be immutable.

---

### FR-ADM-008 — Restaurant Configuration

The system shall support restaurant-specific configuration including:

* Restaurant name
* Address
* Tax identification number
* Contact information
* Logo
* Receipt header and footer
* Thermal printer settings

---

## Business Rules

* Business configuration shall be data-driven whenever possible.
* Administrators shall not modify historical operational data directly.
* Soft deletion shall be preferred over permanent deletion for operational entities.
* Every administrative action affecting business behavior shall be auditable.
* The system shall be designed to support future multi-branch operation without requiring major architectural changes.

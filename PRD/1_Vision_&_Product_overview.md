# 1. Vision & Product Overview

## Vision

Develop a modern Restaurant Management System specifically designed for high-volume, fast-casual restaurants where operational efficiency is the primary competitive advantage.

The system must streamline the complete restaurant workflow, from order creation to payment, minimizing delays between departments while providing full visibility into restaurant operations.

The application should serve as the operational backbone of the restaurant, allowing staff to perform their daily tasks with the fewest possible interactions and reducing the cognitive load during peak hours.

---

## Product Overview

The Restaurant Management System is a web-based application optimized for tablets and desktop devices used by restaurant personnel.

The system manages the complete operational lifecycle of a customer order:

* Order creation
* Kitchen preparation
* Delivery tracking
* Payment processing
* Inventory management
* Production management
* Reporting and analytics

Unlike generic Point-of-Sale systems, this application is designed around the operational needs of high-volume restaurants where preparation speed, communication between staff, and inventory traceability are more valuable than complex accounting features.

The system replaces manual communication between waiters and kitchen personnel with real-time synchronized workflows, reducing service time and operational errors.

---

## Product Principles

Every feature should follow these principles:

### Speed First

Every screen should minimize the number of taps required to complete a task.

The application should favor fast interactions over feature richness.

---

### Operational Simplicity

Restaurant employees should require minimal training.

Interfaces should expose only the information required for the current task.

---

### Real-Time Synchronization

Changes performed by one employee should immediately become visible to every other relevant employee.

Examples include:

* New orders
* Kitchen status updates
* Delivery updates
* Payment status
* Inventory changes

---

### Reliability

No confirmed order should ever be lost.

The application should continue operating correctly even during temporary connectivity interruptions whenever technically feasible.

---

### Traceability

Every important business event should be auditable, including:

* Inventory movements
* Order modifications
* Order cancellations
* Payments
* Administrative actions

---

### Extensibility

The system should be designed to support future capabilities without requiring major architectural changes.

Examples include:

* Multiple restaurant branches
* Delivery integrations
* Loyalty programs
* QR ordering
* Online ordering
* Kitchen analytics
* Additional user roles

# 3. Goals and Success Metrics

## Primary Goals

### Goal 1

Reduce the time required to create and confirm customer orders.

**Success metrics**

* Average order creation time.
* Average number of interactions required to complete an order.

---

### Goal 2

Reduce communication delays between kitchen personnel and waiters.

**Success metrics**

* Average preparation time.
* Average time between "Ready" and "On the Way."
* Average time between "On the Way" and "Delivered."

---

### Goal 3

Improve operational visibility.

Managers should be able to determine the current restaurant status in real time.

**Success metrics**

* Number of active orders.
* Orders by status.
* Kitchen workload.
* Pending payments.
* Inventory alerts.

---

### Goal 4

Improve inventory accuracy.

Every inventory movement should have a business justification.

**Success metrics**

* Percentage of inventory movements linked to business events.
* Number of manual adjustments required.
* Inventory discrepancies detected during physical counts.

---

### Goal 5

Support restaurant growth.

The application should support future operational expansion without requiring redesign.

Potential future capabilities include:

* Multiple restaurant locations.
* Larger dining areas.
* Delivery services.
* QR ordering.
* Customer loyalty.
* Advanced analytics.

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The system should collect sufficient information to calculate operational KPIs.

### Order KPIs

* Orders created today.
* Average ticket value.
* Average preparation time.
* Average delivery time.
* Average payment completion time.
* Average total order lifecycle.
* Orders canceled.
* Orders modified after confirmation.

---

### Kitchen KPIs

* Active preparation queue.
* Average preparation duration.
* Longest waiting order.
* Peak preparation periods.

---

### Waiter KPIs

* Orders delivered.
* Average delivery time.
* Orders currently awaiting delivery.

---

### Inventory KPIs

* Inventory turnover.
* Daily ingredient consumption.
* Production quantities.
* Low-stock products.
* Waste by product.
* Inventory adjustments.

---

### Financial KPIs

* Daily sales.
* Sales by payment method.
* Sales by product category.
* Sales by product.
* Operating expenses.
* Gross operational balance.

---
